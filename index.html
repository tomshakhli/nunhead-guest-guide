At a glance

Essentials for arrival and everyday use

The details guests usually want first, all in one place: heating, parking, contact information and a few quick practical notes.

Parking

There is space for a car in the driveway. Please remember that the road in front of the house is one-way — turn left out of the house.

Heating

The heating is controlled by a thermostat in the living room next to the shelving unit. It's set to 18°C — feel free to adjust to a comfortable temperature for your stay.

Contact

If anything is unclear or something needs attention, please message us on the WhatsApp thread we set up before your arrival.

Tom and Polly's mobile numbers, plus Polly's sister Beth (15 minutes away, holds a spare set of keys), are all in that thread for easy reference.

Useful numbers

Quick house basics

  • Cafetière for coffee on the worktop

  • Dishwasher tablets under the sink

Getting here

  • Nunhead station (7-min walk): direct trains to Victoria and Blackfriars
  • Queen's Road Peckham (10-min walk): direct to London Bridge and Shoreditch
  • Peckham Rye (15-min walk): the area's main station, well connected across central London
  • From Gatwick: direct train to London Bridge, then change to Queen's Road Peckham. Or direct train to Clapham Junction and change to Peckham Rye.
  • From Heathrow: Piccadilly line and change, or an Uber if you have bags
House guide

Practical notes for the house

The notes below cover the details that are most useful once you are in and settled. Open whichever section you need.

Kitchen
  • The kitchen is fully equipped — please help yourself to all appliances and utensils.
  • Cafetière for coffee is on the worktop.
  • Feel free to use the spices, oils etc — if you use anything excessively, please replace it.
  • The smoke and carbon monoxide detector is above the fridge.
Laundry
  • Detergent and fabric softener are already in the washing machine — it has an automatic dispenser, so no need to add more.
  • Best settings for most washes: Cotton 30 or Eco 40-60.
  • If you use the dryer, please empty the water container in the top left corner after each cycle.
Dishwasher
  • Tablets are in the cupboard under the sink.
  • Turn the power on, press start, and close the door. If it doesn't start, press start again.
Rubbish and recycling

There are 4 bins in the kitchen:

  • Top left: nappies
  • Top right: food
  • Bottom left: general waste
  • Bottom right: recycling (cardboard, plastic, metal)

When full, take them out to the larger bins:

  • Green bin: general waste
  • Blue bin: recycling
  • Brown bin: food

Collection day is Wednesday. The bin crew leaves them at the top of the driveway — please bring them back next to the bike shed.

Living room and entertainment
  • The TV has BBC iPlayer, ITV Hub, 4OD, Netflix, Prime, YouTube. Turn on and the menu appears at the bottom of the screen — select an app to open.
Bedrooms
  • Main bedroom: kingsize double bed. Ceiling fan operated by remote control.
  • Second bedroom: cot and a double airbed.
  • Third bedroom: single bed.

If you open windows, please remember to close them when you go out.

Bathroom
  • Two switches outside the bathroom: one for the light, one for the extractor fan.
  • Please use the extractor fan when showering — it runs for 10 minutes after switching off.
  • There's a step to help reach the window if needed.
Garden
  • Cushions for outdoor chairs are in the kitchen by the bifold doors — please take them out and use the seating.
  • Outdoor dining table and chairs are under a protective cover.
  • Check the weather: if rain isn't forecast, fine to leave cushions out — otherwise please bring them back inside.
Babies & children
  • Toys are in the living room in a basket next to the window.
  • Children's books are on the bottom shelf in the living room, and in the second and third bedrooms.
  • Safety locks are fitted on several cupboards in the living room and kitchen.

Explore Nunhead & beyond

Easy ideas for a day out

If you're wondering where to start, these routes make for an easy and very Nunhead sort of day.

Peckham Rye and the playground

A 10-minute walk gets you to Peckham Rye Park — big adventure playground, a good cafe, and one of the lovelier green spaces in this part of London.

Open in maps

Nunhead Cemetery

One of London's seven Victorian "Magnificent Seven" cemeteries — atmospheric, overgrown in the best way, and a properly interesting wander.

Open in maps

Telegraph Hill

A nice park with great views back into central London. There's a market 9am–3pm on Saturdays.

Open in maps

Peckham Levels & Bussey Building

A 15-minute walk away — a mix of cool cafes, restaurants, art and music shops, and yoga classes. Recently opened: Peckham Social Sauna, just five minutes from the house. You'll need to book ahead for a weekend slot.

Open in maps

Bellenden Road

A bit further into Peckham — the road to wander for restaurants, cafes, bars and small shops.

Before you leave

Simple checkout reminders

A quick final check before you set off.

  • Put all towels (including kitchen ones) in the washing machine
  • Likewise with the bedding
  • Run the washing machine on Eco 40-60 just before you leave
  • Remove any fresh food from the fridge
  • Make sure all windows are closed
  • Lock the back door
  • Put the keys back in the key box and scramble the code
  • If anything needs attention, please send us a quick message before you go
